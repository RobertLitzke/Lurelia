# Saltmirror Array

A late-summer evening on a salt pan under eleven abandoned radio dishes: a pad-chained straight down the array, a fallen dish ridden as a 45-degree bowl, a slalom round the cactus knolls, a climb through the fynbos to the old shoreline, and the brine flat home.

The track is built from tools/course/maps/saltmirror.track by tools/course/track_file.py; the world round it is this folder's chronicle.
