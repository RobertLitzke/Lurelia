# Lowmouth Bayou

A humid summer morning in the delta, fog on the water: the levee top with the river below, a jump across the breach, blind bends through the cane, railless boardwalks over the cypress swamp and a run through a wrecked steamboat's hull.

The track is built from tools/course/maps/lowmouth.track by tools/course/track_file.py; the world round it is this folder's chronicle.
