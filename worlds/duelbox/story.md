# Duelbox

Not a place. A bench.

Every other world in here is somewhere: it has a history, weather, a reason
the ground is the shape it is. This one is an empty room on a flat shelf,
built to answer one question -- how does this gun fare against that one --
without the map being part of the answer.

Fifty-eight metres by thirty-four, a flat floor, four walls, a roof, six
lamps and a painted line down the middle. Two spawns at opposite ends, forty
metres apart, facing each other. No cover, no height, no pickups, nothing to
fetch. Two bots in here can do nothing but find each other and shoot, which
is the whole of what it is for.

The line on the floor is there so a screenshot says where you are standing.

Run a weapon through it with `--duel`; see `docs/SHOOTER.md`.
