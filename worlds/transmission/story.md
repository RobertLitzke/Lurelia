# Transmission

A relay station dug into a glacier, built to pass power down the ice to
somewhere further south and abandoned when that stopped mattering. The
turbine hall in the middle still hums. Everything else is corridor, three
levels of it stacked so close that you can hear a fight going on over your
head and not know how to get there.

Built for deathmatch. Two bridges cross the turbine hall one above the other,
and whoever holds the crossing holds the shield belt; the intake has the
sniper rifle on a ledge with a jump pad up to it; the cold store is the one
quiet room. Every gun in the arsenal is on the floor somewhere, each with its
ammunition beside it.
