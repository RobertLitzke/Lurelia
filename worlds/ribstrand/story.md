# Ribstrand

A kart circuit on a grey northern coast, in Ribshore's light and on
Ribshore's ground: wet sand, grassy dunes, basalt stacks offshore and the
bones of the giants.

One of the titans came ashore here and died along the top of the beach. The
sea and the storms have taken its skull, its spine and its tail; a few of
its teeth stand in the sand, and its ribs still stand in two rows seventy
metres high, curling in over the sand between them. Inland, in the dunes,
lies the skeleton of a calf of its own kind, curled the way it died.

The lap starts as the road leaves the ribcage, heading south down the beach.
It runs half a kilometre along the water's edge on the wet sand, the stacks
out to the right: a blade, a flat block, a needle, an arch, a fin and a stump
in a skirt of boulders. At Strand End it turns inland into the Dune Tangle, a
run of small bends over the ridges past the calf, then north over three
jumps, each laid along a dune crest where it crosses the road at a slant, the
road dipping into the slacks between. Tail Bend swings it west toward the
sea, Shore Bend drops it back onto the beach, and the last straight runs
down the middle of the ribcage to the line.

The track is built from `tools/course/maps/ribstrand.track` by
`tools/course/track_file.py`; the world round it is this folder's chronicle.
