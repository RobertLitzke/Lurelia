# Thornpan Delta

A ring round a dry delta in the cool season, crossing its channels like the spokes of a wheel. The flood comes down during the race: dry sand on the first lap, fords on the second, and on the last the main channel is jumped or waded.

The track is built from tools/course/maps/thornpan.track by tools/course/track_file.py; the world round it is this folder's chronicle.
