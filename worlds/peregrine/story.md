# Peregrine Ridge

A fast, flowing circuit across the chalk downs: a long drafting straight, a banked climb up Falcon Crest, a tight ridge hairpin, a plunge into the Chalk Bowl, and technical S-bends over West Knoll.

The track is built from tools/course/maps/peregrine.track by tools/course/track_file.py; the world round it is this folder's chronicle.
