# Ctfbox

Not a place. A bench, like duelbox, for capture the flag.

One long roofed room, ninety-two metres by thirty-two, on a flat shelf. A flag
stand at each end, three spawns behind each one, two pillars in each half and a
painted line down the middle. Nothing else: no height, no lanes, no pickups. Two
sides in here can only take the other's flag and run it home past whoever is in
the way, so whatever separates two team brains is the team brain and not the map.

Run a match through it with `--ctf`; see `docs/BOTS.md`, "The team".
