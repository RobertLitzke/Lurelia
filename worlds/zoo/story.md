# Zoo

A debugging ground, not a place. Every creature in the library has a pen on a level lawn,
laid out family by family from the north-west corner, with the sea along the south edge for
the fish, whales and shore animals. In a walker's pen each age and sex stands in its own
column, and each row is held at one activity (free, stand, look, graze, drink, sit, lie, walk,
run), so a juvenile lying down or an aged male drinking can be found without waiting for it.
Below the rows each of the species' displays plays over and over. See docs/CREATURES.md, "The zoo".
