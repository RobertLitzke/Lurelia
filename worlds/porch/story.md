# Porch

A timber hall built round a spring that went bad. The people who built it
sat on the deck at the north end in the evenings and looked down over the
floor, which is why everybody still calls the deck the porch and why it is
the first place anybody runs to. The wood has had the hall for a long time
now: moss on every floor, cedar gone dark, ivy out of the corners, and the
spring under the middle of the floor is a mire that burns.

Built for deathmatch. The porch sees everything and everything can see the
porch; the ring of tunnels round the outside is how you get from one side to
the other without being seen from it. Every gun in the arsenal is on the
floor somewhere, each with its ammunition beside it.
